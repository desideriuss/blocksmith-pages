Blocksmith — Data Deletion & Account Removal

Info: Blocksmith uses anonymous accounts. No personally identifiable information (name, email, phone) is collected. However, you may request deletion of your game statistics from our servers.

Data to Be Deleted

Upon your request, the following data will be permanently deleted:

  • Game scores and high scores
  • Total game statistics (lines, combos, levels)
  • Experience points (XP) and badges
  • Profile information (display name, avatar preference)
  • Friend list
  • Leaderboard records
⚠️ Warning: This action cannot be undone. Deletion will be completed within 30 days.
